A few useful answers

Before you take a seat.

Who is this for?+

Business owners and small teams who want AI agents built for how they actually work, and more of them than the $20 membership’s shared request queue covers. You don’t need to be a member first: the full Vault is included. Not ready for a seat? Start free in the Gallery, on the Newsstand, or with the decision logs on Substack.

How long does a build take?+

In the $20 shared queue, half of all requests reached their member within 6 days, and that includes the wait in line. Your builds skip that line: work starts the day you post, and my agents scope each one with you first so you know what you’re getting and roughly when.

What if I don’t use both builds in a month?+

They don’t roll over. Fixes and changes to the agents we’ve already built for you are always included, so a quiet month still has work in it.

What if an agent is too big for one build?+

We tell you in the channel before starting. A big agent can use both of a month’s builds, or be split into parts across months. You decide.

What counts as a custom agent?+

A new agent built for your business, on your AI and apps. For example an inbox agent wired to your Gmail, or a weekly report agent for your CRM. Fixes and changes to agents we’ve already built for you don’t count toward your two.

Is there a call to get started?+

No. Right after you pay you’ll get an email from me. Tell us where to send your Slack invite, accept it, and post the first agent you want. Everything happens in Slack, on your schedule.

Do I need Slack?+

Yes. We send a Slack Connect invite, so your channel sits right inside your own Slack. Anyone you add from your side can post requests too.

Who runs the AI?+

You do, on your own accounts. We set the agents up there and help you connect what they need, so nothing about your business lives with us.

How do I cancel?+

It’s month to month with no contract. Reply to any of my emails or post in your channel and I’ll cancel it before your next charge, so you’re never charged again.
