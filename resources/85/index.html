    03

    Build

    Copy block 2 · page 3

    Set up a follow-up system for me in Notion.

1. Create a Notion page called "Give Engine" with three databases

   inside it. If it already exists, use it and don't duplicate.

   - Content: Article ID, Title, URL, Summary, Published (date)

   - Leads: Lead ID, Name, Email, Company, Notes, Pain points,

     Last contact (date), Status (open or closed)

   - Gives: Give ID, Lead ID, Article ID, Score, Confidence,

     Status (scored, unavailable, drafted or sent), Scored on,

     Drafted on, Sent on (dates)

2. Fill Content from the links, sitemap or blog address I give you.

   Open every piece and write a 2-3 sentence Summary of the specific

   problem it helps a reader solve, in plain words. Give each one the

   next Article ID after the highest in the table (A001, A002...),

   oldest first. Skip any URL already in Content.

3. Fill Leads from the export, list or names I give you, each with the

   next Lead ID (L001, L002...). Status is open unless I say the deal

   is done. Pain points: if my notes name a worry or obstacle, use

   that; otherwise read my Gmail threads with that person and write

   the 1-3 problems they're trying to solve, in their words. If you

   find none, write "unknown". Last contact: the date of the latest

   email between us in Gmail, or empty if there is none.

4. Tell me how many articles and leads you added, and list every lead

   whose Pain points are "unknown".
    04

    Build

    give-new-lead

    Create a skill called give-new-lead. When I give you a lead (a name,

an email, or a forwarded thread), do this (save these steps into

the skill word for word):

1. Catch up: for each Gives row with Status "drafted", search my Gmail

   Sent mail for an email to that lead sent on or after Drafted on.

   If there is one, set Status to "sent", Sent on to its date, and the

   lead's Last contact to its date.

2. Find the lead in Leads by email. If they're missing, add them with

   the next Lead ID, Status open, and Pain points found the same way

   as in setup. If their Pain points are "unknown", stop and ask me

   what they're struggling with, save my answer as their Pain

   points, and continue.

3. If give_score.py doesn't exist yet, write a Python script called

give_score.py that takes a list of (Lead ID, Article ID) pairs.

   Install the typesafe-sdk package. For each pair, call Jev once:

   client = TypeSafeClient(model="jev-1.13.0"), which reads the key

   from TYPESAFE_API_KEY, then

   answers = client.system_one(state=..., questions={"fit":

   Score(...)}).answers.

   State = {"lead_pain_points", "lead_notes", "article_title",

   "article_summary"}. The Score question: instructions "Would this

   article genuinely help this person with the problems they

   described?", criteria, lowest first: "No, it is about something

   else", "Same topic, but it does not help with their actual

   problem", "Partly helps with one of their problems", "Clearly

   helps with one of their main problems", "Directly answers their

   biggest problem; they would want it today". Score out of 10 =

   round(answers["fit"].score / 4 * 10, 1); Confidence =

   answers["fit"].confidence. Run 10 calls at a time. If a call

   still fails after the SDK's retries, mark it unavailable and

   leave Score empty (a failed call is never a 0).

4. Ask me for my Jev API key. Set it as TYPESAFE_API_KEY only inside

   the command that runs the script. Never print it, save it, or put

   it in the skill.

5. Score every article in Content for this lead. Save one Gives row

   per pair: Give ID = Lead ID-Article ID (e.g. L014-A007), Score,

   Confidence, Scored on today, and Status "scored" or "unavailable"

   (if the row is already drafted or sent, keep that Status).

6. Show me the top 5 (score, confidence, title), leaving out any

   article already drafted or sent to this lead.

7. If the top one scores above 7 with Confidence of at least 0.7,

   write a Gmail draft from me with it, never sent: one line on why

   I thought of them, tied to their problem in their words, the link,

   a sign-off. Under 90 words, no "just checking in", no ask. Set

   that Gives row to "drafted", Drafted on today. If nothing clears

   that bar, tell me which topic would have helped them.

Never send an email, even if Gmail offers to.
    05

    Build

    give-new-article

    Create a skill called give-new-article. When I give you a link to

something I just published, do this (save these steps into the

skill word for word):

1. Catch up on sent drafts exactly as in give-new-lead step 1.

2. Add the piece to Content if it's missing: next Article ID, Title,

   URL, a 2-3 sentence Summary of the problem it solves, Published.

3. Find quiet leads: Status open, Pain points not "unknown", Last

   contact empty or 14+ days ago, and no Gives row for this article

   that is scored, drafted or sent. If any of them emailed me in the last 14

   days, update their Last contact and leave them out.

4. Score this article for each quiet lead with give_score.py (if it

   doesn't exist, write it exactly as step 3 of the give-new-lead

   skill says), asking for my key the same way. Save Gives rows the same way.

5. Everyone with a Score above 7 and Confidence of at least 0.7 gets

   it. Show me that list first: name, score, the pain point it hits.

6. For each of them, write a personal Gmail draft from me, never

   sent: one line in their words about their problem, the link, a

   sign-off, under 90 words, no ask, no two alike. Set their Gives

   row to "drafted", Drafted on today.

7. Tell me how many leads were scored, how many were unavailable,

   and how many drafts are waiting in Gmail.
